01 //About Me
Sri Lanka — Available Globally

I like both sides of the same problem.

I’m Adsayan, a Software Engineering student. I like both sides of a digital product — writing the code behind it, and thinking about how it feels to use. Most of my time goes into web apps, and just as much into how those apps look and feel.

I started out interested in UI/UX and visual design, and slowly drifted into the engineering side. Now I enjoy both — building web apps, trying out interface ideas, and turning something vague into something that actually works. I’m still learning, but I learn best by building things.

Philosophy

A clean interface and clean code turn out to be the same kind of problem. I like both to be simple.

Commitment

I’m still a student. Most of what I know, I learned by building something and then fixing it.
